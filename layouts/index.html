Royal Griffin candidates 02, 03 and 04 are rejected respectively for chest-feather defects, planar side review and chest mesh holes; it now moves to component-model reconstruction; Swordsman candidate 02’s holding, three attacks and walk now pass local review, while Pikeman’s separate body/pike two-hand constraint and 10-frame lunge now pass; Halberdier now passes three attack directions, hit and defence with its original long-weapon components. Angel and Archangel use a separate sword layer: Angel passes flight, three sword directions, hit, defence and move transitions, while Archangel passes flight, three sword directions, defence, hit and move transitions in front and side review. All of this remains private Blender review work, with no export or in-game integration.
