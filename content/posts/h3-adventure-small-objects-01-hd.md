The 24 small adventure-map objects from `AVA0077` through `AVA0100` now have HD artwork in the game, including trinkets, scrolls, cloaks and magical objects. Their tiny canvas makes original pixel edges especially visible on a high-resolution map.

![Small objects first batch: original and HD comparison](/images/h3-environment-hd/adventure-small-objects-01-hd-compare.png)

Every result starts from its original artwork and is constrained back to its native transparent canvas. Silhouette, scale, footprint, click area and draw order therefore remain original; only restrained material detail is added. Full 2×, 3× and 4× resource validation reports zero errors.
