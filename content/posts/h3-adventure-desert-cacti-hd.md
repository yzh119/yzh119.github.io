The desert cactus set is now complete: `AVLCA010`–`AVLCA130`, plus the two rotated variants `AVLCA1R0` and `AVLCA2R0`. Each resource was painted around its own source form—columnar, clustered, branching, or low-growing—rather than being reduced to one generic attractive cactus.

![Desert cactus HD comparison](/images/h3-environment-hd/desert-cactus-variants-hd-compare.png)

The final images remain constrained by the original transparency and footprint, so map placement, pathing and occlusion are unchanged. **223** adventure-map body resources are now customized, and the complete resource check reports 0 errors.
