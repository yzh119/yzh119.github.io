Six pure land-rock resources, `AVLRK1S0`, `AVLRK2S0`, `AVLRK3D0`, `AVLRK3S0`, `AVLRK4S0` and `AVLRK5D0`, now have HD body art. Each retains the original rock count, crack outline, separation gaps and moss placement, so map occlusion and passability boundaries do not change.

![Land rock HD comparison](/images/h3-environment-hd/land-rocks-hd-compare.png)

Water-edge rocks are deliberately excluded: their waterline remains an original layer until the dedicated shoreline pass. There are now **181** customized body resources, and the complete adventure-asset check remains at 0 errors.
