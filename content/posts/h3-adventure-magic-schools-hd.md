This batch focuses on the three adventure-map magic schools. They appear on land, water and underground, with substantially different original silhouettes, so one illustration is not reused for all of them.

## Three biomes

The land version retains its octagonal masonry base and tiered round tower; the water version keeps its stilted timber hall, roof dome and water posts; the underground version retains its sharp crystal rock and entrance. All three update only the visible body while keeping the original canvas, shadow, occlusion layers and interaction definitions.

![Magic schools across three biomes](/images/h3-environment-hd/magic-schools-compare.png)

Assets are synced into the local private mod’s 2×, 3× and 4× layers. No VCMI source was modified.
