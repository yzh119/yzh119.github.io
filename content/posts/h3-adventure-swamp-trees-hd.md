Swamp vegetation begins with two small tree forms. The repaints retain the original compact silhouettes, twisted trunks, wet blue-green shadows and low footprint; leaf layering is clearer without turning them into ordinary woodland or tropical palms.

![HD swamp-tree comparison](/images/h3-environment-hd/swamp-trees-hd-compare.png)

Both bodies are constrained by the native transparency silhouette. Canvas, shadow, occlusion and hit template stay unchanged, and the assets are installed at 2×, 3× and 4×. The complete adventure-resource check reports zero errors, with **142** customized body resources.

Two medium swamp groves followed, retaining the source art's wet blue-green shadows, low irregular canopies and root gaps. Each body remains clipped to its native transparency silhouette.

![HD swamp-grove comparison](/images/h3-environment-hd/swamp-groves-hd-compare.png)

Customized body resources now total **144**.
